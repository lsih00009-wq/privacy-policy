Privacy Policy · Last updated: 2 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game SHAHMAT — Chess ("the app", "we") handles information. By using the app you agree to this policy.

1. Information we do not collect

The app does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or home address. We do not collect your contacts, photos, files, or precise location.

2. Information stored on your device

Game progress, settings, board themes, match history and saved games are stored locally on your device only. They are not uploaded to us and are removed when you uninstall the app.

3. Advertising

The app may show advertisements provided by third-party advertising partners such as Google AdMob. These partners may collect a device advertising identifier, approximate (non-precise) location derived from your IP address, and technical information about your device in order to serve and measure ads. This data is handled under their own privacy policies: policies.google.com/privacy and AdMob & data.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time from your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

4. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will act on it.

5. Security

Because the app keeps your game data on your own device and does not run user accounts on our servers, we hold no personal database to protect. Any data transmitted by advertising partners is sent over encrypted connections.

6. Your rights

You may stop all data processing by uninstalling the app. To ask a question or request deletion of any data associated with you, contact us at the address below.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above will change, and the current version will always be available at this page.

8. Contact
